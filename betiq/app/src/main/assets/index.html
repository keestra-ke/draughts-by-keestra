Responsible Use

This tracker does not change the mathematics of betting. Negative-progression systems like Labouchere increase risk of large losses in exchange for small, frequent wins.

Set strict limits, take breaks, and never bet more than you can afford to lose.

Settings

Four decisions: your money, the bet, the escrow seed, and your guards.

1 · Money
Plan capital is the fixed baseline every percentage is measured against. Active is the cash you can actually stake. Escrow is ring-fenced for gap cover and is never profit.

Minimum 3,000. This is the denominator for unit size, loss limit and target — not a wallet balance. Changing it re-bases those percentages and re-seeds active cash only when no cycle is live.
Minimum 100 KSh per move, and you review before it posts. This is manual cash only — it is never betting profit. The automatic deposit quoted inside a gap dialog is separate and has no 100 KSh floor, because it is sized to the exact shortfall.
2 · Betting
The Labouchere line and how the first stake sizes the unit. Both are locked once a cycle starts.
1–8 digits, each 1–9 (example 12232). No zeros. Each win peels the two outer digits off; each loss appends the sum.

A stake above your active balance is a gap: the gap dialog then asks you to cover it from escrow and/or a deposit. This is the number to sanity-check before you commit.
3 · Rollover sub-engine
Replaces the single ~2.00 bet with a two-leg rollover at 1.42. Record one leg result per day: a leg-1 loss settles the pair as L; a leg-1 win rolls its return into leg 2, which settles as W+W (win, effective 2.0164) or W+L (loss). The line rules are unchanged.
Each leg is priced at 1.42 and the pair settles at 2.0164 effective. Not editable mid-pair.
4 · Escrow
Gap-cover reserve only. Escrow is never included in profit, ROI, or cycle P/L. This amount is the only source of escrow: it is seeded into each new cycle, spent when a gap bet is placed, and returned only if that gap bet wins. No win or outcome ever adds to it.
Re-seeded to this value at the start of every cycle — whatever was left is released to active cash when a cycle ends. Size it against the stakes above: a seed smaller than your largest stake will still leave you paying a deposit. Never counted as betting profit.
5 · Guards & pace
Targets, stops and timing all read betting results only — deposits and escrow inflows never count toward them.
Prompt when cycle betting P/L reaches this % of plan capital.
No bet can be placed or settled before this time. A hard gate on the engine, not a reminder — there is nothing to skip or override.
6 · Cloud sync
Sign in to back up your ledger and open it on another device. Offline changes sync when you reconnect. Your data stays on this device until you sign in.
